# Competitor scan

- **Date:** 2026-10-14
- **Purpose:** Light context for choosing the idea (see [ADR-001](../adr/001-problem-and-idea.md)). Deliberately limited to a couple of sources, not a market study.

## Boundless

See the [company profile](boundless-company-profile.md) for what Boundless offers and to whom. The Boundless site does not mention AI features.

## Blue Light Card (primary source: its homepage)

- £4.99 for two years; 15,000+ brand partners, online and in-store.
- Discovery: search by store or brand, category browsing, "offers near you".
- Eligibility verified by work email, or by uploading proof.
- The page describes no personalisation or recommendation features and no AI.

## Roundup of public-sector schemes (secondary source: HouseholdMoneySaving)

Compares MyLifestyle, Boundless, CSSC, PSdiscounts and Blue Light Card. Findings relevant here (author's opinion, not measured data):

- No single scheme covers everyone; savings are spread across several schemes and portals.
- Boundless "only pays off if you use the attraction perks".
- A staff discount is not always the cheapest price, so members still compare.

## What this does and does not show

**Established:** the sources above say what they say.

**Inference (not validated with users):** the gap is less about catalogue size and more about helping a member decide what to use and whether the membership is worth its fee. A natural-language finder fits the first part; a savings estimate against the fee addresses the second.

**Limits:**
- Only landing pages and one roundup were read; Boundless's own app, search and member area were not examined.
- No member interviews or usage data.
- Prices and offers change; figures are as of the date above.
